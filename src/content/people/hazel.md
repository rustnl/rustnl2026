Hazel spends her days working on building out teams of humans as well as the infrastructure, systems, and tooling to make life better for others.
She’s worked at a variety of companies and knows that the hardest problems to solve are the social ones.
One of her favorite things is watching someone light up when they understand something for the first time, and a goal of hers is to help as many people as possible experience that joy.
She also loves swing dancing, both as a leader and a follower.
