Pete LeVasseur guides Rust adoption efforts at Woven by Toyota and
its suitability for the highest levels of safety-criticality. He’s also lead of
the Safety-Critical Rust Consortium where there’s a broad base of
support for working to make Rust a suitable first-language choice for
safety-critical domains such as Automotive, Aerospace, and Industrial.
Pete is co-lead of the Rust Project’s Content Team, a member of the
Rust Vision Doc Team and loves interviewing those that are doing
interesting things with Rust.
