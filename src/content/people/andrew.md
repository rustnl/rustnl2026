Andrew has been a member of the Rust project on the libs-api team since 2015. He
has also worked on building and maintaining core ecosystem crates such as regex,
jiff, memchr, bstr, csv, ignore, walkdir and more. He wrote ripgrep, which
searches code repositories very quickly and powers the search functionality
of many code editors, such as VS Code. Finally, Andrew is currently working on
improving Python developer tooling at Astral.

Andrew lives in Massachusetts with his Wife, Kaitlyn, and son, Murphy. He likes
running, reading, cigars and coding.
