I started with Rust during university in 2021 and quickly fell in love with the
language because of its type and trait system. In early 2022, I began my
contributions to Rust for Linux, providing a solution to pinned initialization,
which I maintain to this day. I then continued to review core Rust code and
involved myself more with the development of Rust itself. Now I'm working
mostly on field projections, in-place initialization, and various other
language features.
