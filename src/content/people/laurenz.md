Laurenz is primary maintainer of the Typst language and compiler.
He started Typst with a friend during his CS bachelor's, wrote his master's thesis about it, and then went on to co-found the Typst company.
Between language design, compiler implementation, and code review, he hacks away at the Typst web app, figuring out why WebKit broke our multi-threaded WebAssembly setup (again).
