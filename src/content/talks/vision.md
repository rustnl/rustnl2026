Over the past fifteen years, Rust has grown from a small project used by
Mozilla and few others, to being used by millions of developers. But our
communication patterns haven't scaled well with that growth. In this talk,
we'll present some of the initiatives that the Rust Project is undertaking, and
participating in, in order to reshape the way we listen to and collaborate with
our users. Structured efforts like the Vision Doc and User Research teams, the
Project Goals process, and the Content Team, as well as ongoing collaborations
such as with the Rust for Linux and Rust for CPython projects, each serve as
tools to and lessons in how to communicate with and listen more effectively to
our users.
