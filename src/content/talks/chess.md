We’ll cover some of the most interesting or unique problems we encounter writing competitive chess engines and how Rust is excellently suited for solving these problems, 
enabling us to write very fast, portable code while sparing us from many errors that are nearly impossible to debug. 
We’ll discuss the implementation of sub-microsecond neural network inference, lock- and wait-free data structures that can be mutated by hundreds of threads in parallel, and our favorite tricks and contrivances.
