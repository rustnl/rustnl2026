As it turns out, handling units of measurement in the real world is actually really hard!
uom is an awesome library that provides strong, static types to represent different measurement units. 
It's tempting to just slap uom types on everything and call it a day, but when making production CAD software, this isn't enough. 
Today we'll be talking about all of the surprising challenges with real world unit handling, and investigating some solutions and their tradeoffs.
