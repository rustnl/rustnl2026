Across The Netherlands, the energy transition is driving major upgrades to our digital infrastructure. I’ll share how our team chose Rust for the application layer of the country’s next-generation smart meters, and how that decision helped us win a competitive RFP. We’ll look at why Rust has proven to be such a strong fit for demanding requirements around security, energy efficiency, and long-term flexibility—and what that could mean for organizations considering Rust for their own critical projects.
