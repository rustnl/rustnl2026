The journey to large scale Rust adoption begins with not just a single
step, but many parallel steps. Toyota’s approach focused on two
things: (1) building the foundational toolchains required for
safety-critical applications; and (2) getting the organization
comfortable with shipping a new language into production vehicles.
Toyota’s journey reached a milestone this year with shipping Rust in
production vehicles worldwide. Rust powers the stable compatibility
layer that enables digital cockpit user experiences. Here we’ll discuss
some of the rationale behind starting with non-safety-critical areas as
a solid way to build up confidence and momentum before delving into
safety-critical Rust in your organization!
