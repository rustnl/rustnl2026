David Brown has been with Linaro since 2015, with most of that time spent working with the Zephyr RTOS, with a focus on embedded device security. He is the security chair of the Zephyr project, and sits on both the technical steering committee, and the Zephyr Governing Board. He also is a maintainer of the MCUboot secure bootloader. He has been programming in Rust since before 1.0 (before 2015), and did a majority of the work bringing Rust support to the Zephyr project.
