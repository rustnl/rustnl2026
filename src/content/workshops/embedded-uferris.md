Rather than focusing solely on peripheral demos, the workshop teaches participants how to continue on their own after the workshop. It is targeted at Rust embedded beginners who are curious about embedded development and want a practical, realistic path into the embedded Rust ecosystem.

Using uFerris and esp-hal as a concrete platform, participants will learn how to set up projects, navigate the embedded Rust ecosystem, read documentation effectively, and adapt existing examples to their own needs.
