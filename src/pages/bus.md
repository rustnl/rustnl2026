RustWeek has arranged buses to take you from Utrecht city centre to [DUS](/dus).
DUS is the venue for the [unconference](/unconf-intro) and All-Hands, from Thu May 21 to Sat May 23.

The buses depart from the **Hotel NH Utrecht** and will take you to DUS in 20 minutes.

## Schedule for all days

Two buses will depart from **NH Utrecht Hotel** to **DUS**, at **08:30**.

A small bus (20p) will depart from NH at the top of the hour:<br /> 9:00, 10:00, 11:00, 12:00, 13:00, 14:00, 15:00 and 16:00.

---

Two buses will depart from **DUS** to **NH Utrecht Hotel**, one at **17:30** and one at **18:30**.

A small bus (20p) will depart from DUS at the half hour:<br /> 9:30, 10:30, 11:30, 12:30, 13:30, 14:30, 15:30 and 16:30.

## Bus stop location

Bus stop address: [Jaarbeursplein 24, 3521 AR Utrecht](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Sm2JfHWFm89Lu5Bp8)

If you travel via Utrecht Central Station:
- take the exit "Jaarbeurszijde" from the station
- walk (for 200m) to the bus stop:
