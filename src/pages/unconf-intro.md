The RustWeek 2026 unconference is an invite-only event for Rust maintainers, held from Thu May 21 to Sat May 23, in parallel to the Rust All Hands.

The goal of the unconference is to facilitate maintainer groups to work together in-person, including cross-collaboration with other maintainer groups and with members of Rust project teams.

The unconf is part of [RustWeek 2026](/), held in Utrecht, The Netherlands from May 18 - 23.

## The unconf groups

The unconference is for Rust maintainers of these groups:

- Bevy Engine
- Linebender
- Rust Embedded Working Group
- Safety-Critical Rust Consortium
- ... your group? [contact us!](mailto:unconf@rustnl.org)
