RustWeek will return to beautiful Utrecht in 2027, welcoming over 900+ community members, professionals and the maintainers of Rust.

The world's biggest Rust conference includes one day of workshops, two days of talks, a hackathon, social activities, and more!

Check out the RustWeek 2027 website on [https://2027.rustweek.org/](2027.rustweek.org) or get your tickets directly via [onliveevent.nl/rustweek-2027](https://event.onliveevent.nl/).
