Our workshop line-up is complete with a RustWeek-record 8 workshops! With topics ranging from web and gaming to embedded and rustc we hope to have something on offer for everyone.

We have a couple of returning friends, Alice & François, Georg and Matthias but also a lot of new faces.

The full line-up:

- [Bevy in Action: Build, Polish, and Deploy a Match‑Three Game](/workshops/#bevy-in-action) by Alice Ivy Cecile and François Mockers
- [Building Web applications with Diesel](/workshops/#diesel) by Georg Semmler
- [Empowered by the Ecosystem: Learning Embedded Rust with uFerris](/workshops/#embedded-uferris) by Omar Hiari
- [How to learn hard things](/workshops/#hard-problems) by Jynn Nelson
- [Integrate Rust into existing (embedded) C applications](/workshops/#integrate-rust-into-c) by Christian Meusel
- [Embedded Development With Rust on Zephyr](/workshops/#rust-on-zephyr) by David Brown
- [Crafting errors like rustc](/workshops/#rustc) by Esteban Kuber & Jana Dönszelmann
- [Writing Better Rust: Fully Using The Type System](/workshops/#writing-better-rust) by Matthias Endler

All workshops take place on **Monday May 18**, the day before the conference, from 10:00 to 17:00.

The full list with abstracts can be found [here](/workshops/).
