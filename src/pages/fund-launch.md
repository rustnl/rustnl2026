# Funding Open-Source Maintainers

Join us to celebrate the start of NLnet's efforts to deliver structural support for
load-bearing leadership and maintenance roles in the free and open source community.

This event marks the launch of [NLnet's Rust Maintainers Fund](https://nlnet.nl/maintainers/rust/),
an initiative together with RustNL to provide sustainable, long-term support for Rust maintainers,
helping them continue the essential (and often invisible) work that powers the entire Rust ecosystem.

## About the Maintainers Fund

NLnet's Rust Maintainers Fund aims to provide sustainable, long-term support for
Rust maintainers: the people who review code, fix bugs, maintain
infrastructure, and keep the ecosystem healthy.

By supporting maintainers directly, the fund helps ensure the continued success
and reliability of Rust for everyone.

Learn more: https://nlnet.nl/maintainers/rust/
