The workshops of RustWeek 2026 will be held in **Jaarbeurs Beatrixgebouw**. This venue is located in the center of Utrecht, right next to the Central Station.

The address of the venue is:

> **Jaarbeurs Beatrixgebouw\
> Jaarbeursplein 6\
> 3521 CA Utrecht\
> Nederland**

Check out our  [Getting There](/getting-there) page for instructions for getting to the conference venue, which is located in the same area as the workshop venue.

The most convenient method to get there is by train, since it's next to the train station.

## Accessibility

The venue is fully accessible by wheelchair. Every room has wheelchair spaces. There are also elevators to every room that is part of the conference. Accessible toilets are also present.
