Are you a (Rust) meetup organizer or dreaming of becoming one? Join this session and get to know other meetup organizers!

The meetup is on the first day of the conference at Kinepolis, during the first break, in the Reception room. The room is located across the entrance of the main stage on the 2nd floor.

Please note, that you need a conference ticket to access the venue.

Details and signup on [meetup.com](https://www.meetup.com/rust-nederland/events/314750232)

**What to expect / possible goals:**

- Get advice on how to start your local Rust Meetup
- Meet and network with other Rust meetup organizers from different cities and countries
- Share experiences, successes, and challenges in running local Rust communities
- Get insights on promoting diversity, inclusion, and accessibility in your meetup
- Discover tools, resources, and collaboration possibilities between meetups
